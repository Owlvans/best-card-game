EMBERFALL

a duel of three houses

Ember burns. Tide endures. Thorn entangles.
First hero to fall loses the vale.

How to play: Both heroes start at 20 life. Energy starts at 1, grows +1 each turn (cap 10) and refills.

Click a card (or press 1–5) to play it. Click your ready creature, then a target, to attack. Enter ends your turn.

Guard must be attacked first. Rush can attack the turn it lands. Spells burn, heal, or draw.
